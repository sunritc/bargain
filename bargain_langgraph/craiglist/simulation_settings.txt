Rules of bargaining:
. B/S can "accept", "propose", "walk away", "stay", "ask"
. If propose, then have to choose an offer
. For S: offer must be greater than buyer offer, but lower than own last offer
. For B: offer must be lower than seller offer, but higher than own last offer


Rules of session:
. fixed maximum number of rounds
. buyer always initiates contact "Hey is this product available?"
. seller responds "Hi. Yes, are you interested? The listing price is ..."
. buyer makes offer for example: "Yes. I can pay $10 for this, does that work for you?"
. negotiation continues for at most 10 rounds (ignore the first deterministic round)

View session from buyer's perspective - buyer is trying to get the price down
Think: Buyer is agent, seller is human


Settings:
. seller is fixed with an emotion, while buyer fixed at another emotion
. seller is neutral initially and then after a certain round, gets angry/impatient, buyer is baseline
